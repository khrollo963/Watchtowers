# 🔺 Angelic Immersion: The Watchtower Database

An interactive, single-file web app exploring the **Enochian Great Table** — the four elemental Watchtowers and the central Tablet of Union received by John Dee and Edward Kelley — rebuilt as a clickable, scryable, fully cross-referenced database.

Click letters directly on the tablet artwork to spell out real angelic names, watch them resolve to live annotations, and dive into a searchable index of the King, Seniors, and 91 Governors of the Aethyrs.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Splash → World flow** — enter through a themed title screen into the Great Table itself.
- **Clickable letter grid** — every letter on the Watchtower artwork is individually tappable, transcribed and pixel-mapped directly from the source image and verified against the canonical Holy Names.
- **Live name-matching ("Scrying the Aethers")** — click letters in sequence and the app checks your growing string against every real name in the database (Holy Names, Kings, Seniors, Governors, Tablet of Union). A match pops an on-image annotation you can click straight into that entry's data sheet.
- **Hover / tap-to-reveal annotations** — desktop hover or mobile double-tap (first tap reveals, second tap enters) on the four Watchtower quadrants, the Tablet of Union cross, and the Black Cross screen.
- **Full Index tab** — searchable tables for all 91 Governors, 24 Seniors, 30 Aethyrs, subtablet quadrants, the Enochian alphabet, and 1584-vs-1587 spelling variants.
- **Support tab** — creator bio and social links.
- **Ambient audio** — an original ambient track plays subtly on entry, with a visible play/pause + volume control.
- **Fully self-contained** — one `.html` file. No build step, no server, no external dependencies (fonts aside). Open it and it works.

## 📖 On accuracy
The letter grid overlaying the Watchtower artwork was transcribed by hand from the source image and cross-checked against the canonical **Linea Spiritus Sancti** (the Three Holy Names row) of each quadrant — every quadrant's center row reads out exactly as `ORO IBAH AOZPI`, `MPH ARSL GAIOL`, `MOR DIAL HCTGA`, and `OIP TEAA PDOCE`, matching the published historical table. The remaining Governor, Senior, and King data comes from standard Enochian source material (Dee's *Liber Scientiae*, the 1587 *Tabula Recensa*, and the Golden Dawn's systematization of the 91 Governors).

Minor transcription discrepancies in less-traveled cells of the grid are possible — if you spot one, please open an issue with the cell location and the correct letter.

## 🛠 Tech

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. No frameworks, no build tools, no dependencies beyond two Google Fonts (Cinzel, Crimson Text) loaded over HTTPS. Runs entirely client-side.
